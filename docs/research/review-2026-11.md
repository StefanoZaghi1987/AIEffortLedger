# Review of Phase 0 — measurements of November 2026

Point-in-time measurements that supported the review of the Phase 0 specifications on 18 November 2026. They come from
[prototype/measure.py](../../prototype/measure.py) on the same Claude Code and Claude Desktop transcripts of one person
as [prototype-findings-2026-11.md](prototype-findings-2026-11.md). Absolute values stay in `prototype/out/` (ADR 0003);
this file gives ratios and is not updated afterwards.

## Human time per person

Human time computed on one stream of the human signals of the person, across all sessions, is 1.061 times human time
computed session by session (idle gap 10 minutes). The difference is time in which the person moved between parallel
sessions: each session alone shows a silence longer than the idle gap, the person shows none. The difference is
larger than the overlap of human time between projects (about 3 %, see the earlier findings), so the split rule must
divide signal gaps between sessions of different projects, not only overlaps.

## Sessions that a person did not start

About 6 % of the sessions have the programmatic entry point `sdk-cli`. Their prompts are 1.5 % of all prompts that the
earlier prototype counted as human signals. The share is small for this person, but every scheduled run, script, or
orchestrator adds to it, so the invocation of a session decides whether its prompts are human signals.

## Limits

- One person, one machine; no orchestrator and no other agent tool in the data.
- Entry points other than `cli`, `local-agent`, and `sdk-cli` do not appear, so their invocation is not observed.
